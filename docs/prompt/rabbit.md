# Rabbit Behavior Image Prompt

## Prompt

A tense vibrant Minecraft promotional art illustration of rabbits fleeing predators at dawn in a carrot farm near a forest edge. The rabbits have brown and white cubic blocky bodies made of flat matte pixelated 16x16 pixel textures with NO reflections, NO shine, NO glossy textures. One central rabbit is captured mid-hop with ears flattened back in terror, blocky dirt particles exploding from behind its kicking hind legs. A blocky orange fox pursues in full sprint, mouth open in a pixelated snarl. Another rabbit stands frozen in alert pose on hind legs near a burrow. A third rabbit dives headfirst into a 1x1 block hole. In the background, a pack of blocky gray wolves waits at the forest edge. All elements are strictly cubic voxel blocks with visible pixelated textures - rows of carrot crops with orange cubes and bright green voxel tops, forest trees made of cube blocks, morning mist rendered as translucent white cube layers. Golden dawn light with warm rays casting long blocky shadows, vibrant saturated browns, whites, oranges and greens. High-fidelity 3D render in official Mojang key art style, flat matte pixelated textures with NO specular highlights, strictly cubic block-based geometry for ALL objects, visible pixel grid on all surfaces, dramatic composition emphasizing survival instinct and escape.
