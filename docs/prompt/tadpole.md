The tadpoles have brown cubic blocky bodies with wiggling rectangular tails made of flat matte pixelated 16x16 pixel textures with NO reflections, NO shine, NO glossy textures. A large swarm of blocky tadpoles swims energetically through the murky water with motion blur on their wiggling tails, several tadpoles closely follow a large blocky green frog parent in a learning formation. All elements are strictly cubic voxel blocks with visible pixelated textures - thick square mangrove roots extend into the brown water, floating blocky lily pads dot the surface, adult blocky frogs sit on distant muddy shores, hanging mangrove leaves create dappled shade. Warm swampy lighting with murky browns and vibrant greens, the scene captures the tadpole lifecycle and swarming behavior. High-fidelity 3D render in official Mojang key art style, flat matte pixelated textures with NO specular highlights, strictly cubic block-based geometry for ALL objects, visible pixel grid on all surfaces, intimate swamp composition emphasizing the tadpole swarm and metamorphosis.
