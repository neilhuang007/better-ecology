# Villager Behavior Image Prompt

## Prompt

A bustling vibrant Minecraft promotional art illustration of a lively village center filled with dynamic villager interactions and activities. The villagers have tan cubic blocky bodies with profession-specific robes made of flat matte pixelated 16x16 pixel textures with NO reflections, NO shine, NO glossy textures. In the foreground, a blocky player character in iron armor holds out a vibrant green cubic emerald to a farmer villager in a brown robe and straw hat who offers a blocky carrot in return mid-trade. Two villagers nearby stand close together gossiping, surrounded by floating green square particle effects above their heads indicating social interaction. A massive blocky iron golem made of iron blocks with vines is mid-swing striking a blocky zombie figure, protecting the village. Another villager in a purple cleric robe sprints away frantically with arms flailing as a green-skinned blocky zombie chases closely behind. All elements are strictly cubic voxel blocks with visible pixelated textures - blocky wooden houses with varied block roofs surround a central stone bell structure, profession workstations like lecterns and smithing tables scatter the square, cobblestone paths connect buildings. Bright sunny village lighting with vibrant saturated colors, the scene captures the social hub atmosphere of a thriving community. High-fidelity 3D render in official Mojang key art style, flat matte pixelated textures with NO specular highlights, strictly cubic block-based geometry for ALL objects, visible pixel grid on all surfaces, busy multi-focal composition emphasizing village life and multiple simultaneous activities.
